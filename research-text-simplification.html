AI-Assisted Text Simplification

How much of a passage’s meaning survives when a language model rewrites it for easier reading?

Comparing a passage with its rewrite

A shorter sentence can be easier to read while saying something different. For example, changing “may cause” to “causes” removes a qualification. Looking at readability alongside meaning helps identify changes that sentence length alone would miss.

My work at ECOLE Lab

As a research assistant, I built Python workflows to compare source passages with AI-generated simplifications. I examined changes in sentence structure, looked for recurring patterns across passages and assessed whether the rewrites preserved the original meaning.
