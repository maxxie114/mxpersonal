Chinese utility-model patent · CN203188726U · 2013

Electronic Remote-Control Lock

A door lock that moves the bolt electronically while keeping the locking mechanism inside the door.

Patent record

How the design works

A conventional door lock exposes a keyhole on the outside. In this design, a remote-controlled actuator moves a mechanical bolt inside the door. A magnetic reed switch enables the actuator when a magnet is brought near the switch.

Publication

The patent lists me as the inventor under my Chinese name, 谢沛熹. I filed the application on April 8, 2013. The utility model was published on September 11, 2013. The patent record includes the original drawing and claims.
