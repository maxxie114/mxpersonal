Electronic Remote-Control Lock

Utility-model patent — CN203188726U · Issued Sep 11, 2013 (now expired)

Designed and patented an electronic remote-control lock that enhances home security by embedding the locking mechanism inside the door and controlling it via a remote signal. The system integrates a reed switch, automotive central lock, and wireless alarm camera, providing hidden, tamper-resistant protection.

Production of English /r/ by Prosodic Position

Poster presentation — Linguistics Undergraduate Research Conference (LURC 4), UC Santa Cruz · Jun 21, 2023

Analyzes how prosodic position affects English /r/ production using machine learning and ultrasound imaging. Used Articulate Assistant Advanced (AAA) to model tongue shape dynamics and applied quantitative methods to visualize and interpret articulatory variation.
