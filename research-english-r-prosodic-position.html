Linguistics Undergraduate Research Conference · UC Santa Cruz (UCSC) · 2023

Production of English /r/ by Prosodic Position

An ultrasound study of how five English speakers shaped their tongues for /r/ at the beginning of phrases, words and syllables.

View poster

What the ultrasound showed

English speakers can produce /r/ by bunching the tongue or curling its tip back. We compared average tongue contours across the three positions. Individual speakers tended to keep a similar shape across positions, even though the shapes differed from speaker to speaker.

The poster and my contribution

Tony Butorovich, Jaye Padgett, Claire Wellwood and I presented this work at UC Santa Cruz. I built Python workflows to automate trials and synchronize speech data. The poster shows the tongue curves and describes how we analyzed the ultrasound recordings with Articulate Assistant Advanced.
