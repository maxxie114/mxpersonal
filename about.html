About Max

Engineer by practice.
Linguist by training.

I’m Peixi “Max” Xie, an AI engineer in San Francisco with a background in speech research and natural language processing.

From speech research to applied AI.

At UC Santa Cruz, I used ultrasound to study how the tongue moves during speech. At San Francisco State University, I earned an M.A. in Linguistics and an M.A. Computational Linguistics Certificate. My graduate research focused on AI-assisted text simplification and how four language models performed on knowledge, reasoning and linguistics tasks.

I’m a nine-time hackathon winner. I use LangChain and Google ADK to prototype agent workflows. For one hackathon, my team used TRM APIs to help explain risk signals in cryptocurrency data. Across these projects, I work with Python, Java and SQL, and use Linux and Docker to run the services.
